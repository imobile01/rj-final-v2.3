RJ-Scheduler for Android - version 2.1 (versionCode 3)
=====================================================

What's included
- SendReceiver: wakes up at 11:59 PM on the 15th and last day of the month.
- SendJob (new): does the actual emailing in the background. Android starts it as soon as
  the phone has internet and retries every 15 minutes (up to about 12 hours) if needed.
- BootReceiver: puts the 11:59 PM alarm back after a phone restart or an app update.
- Notifications: "Records emailed", "Pay period closed", "Couldn't email records".
  Tapping a notification opens the app.

Build (GitHub, the way you did before)
- Upload the files into your repository's RJ-Scheduler folder (see UPDATE steps).
- Actions tab -> the build runs -> download the APK from Artifacts.

IMPORTANT: keep your signing setup
- Do NOT replace your existing build.yml or app/build.gradle if they contain your signing
  key settings. Only update versionCode by hand (see below).

Before giving it to staff, test:
1. Install over the old app (records must still be there).
2. Submit -> Automatic -> "Send test email".
3. Tap "Allow sending while app is closed" -> Allow.
4. Restart the phone, leave the app closed, and confirm the 11:59 PM email arrives.
